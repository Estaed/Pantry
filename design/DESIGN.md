# Pantry design layer

Overrides on Tarik Base v6 (`D:/TarikOS/.brain/skills/tasarim/references/DESIGN.md`); everything
not named here follows the base.

- **Illustration tier: `spot`.** Every food in a list or sheet carries a small drawing (Tarık,
  2026-10-20: "illüstrasyondan kaçınma", "yazıdan çok görsellik"). Drawings are inline SVG, one
  geometry per food, so the app stays light and offline. Style not chosen yet: A thin ink line,
  B flat greys, C flat natural colours (C would bend the one-hue rule inside drawings only; record
  it in `deviations.md` if picked).
- **Hero:** the anchor box holds a ring (kcal eaten against the budget) with kcal left as the
  orange number, and the four meals beside it: the MyNetDiary apple, said in our language.

How it counts

  • Weeks are the seven days ending yesterday, then the seven before, so the last week is always whole and today never drags an average down.
  • A day with nothing logged is left out, not counted as a day under budget; the averages say how many days they rest on.
  • Over is the over tone, as on Today: a day's bar above the 2,200 line, a macro past its target grams, and the big number when the week runs over. Under stays grey; the big number turns orange.
  • Orange is the current place: the last week's date under its grey band in the chart, today in the first week.
  • Weeks compare from seven days logged; before that the chart is the first week filling up, and the day-of-week row waits for two weeks.
